About Me

I grew up just about an hour outside of the Philadelphia area in a sleepy town called Glenmoore. I moved to Philly about 6 years ago now for college, as I studied at Temple University for my undergraduate degree.

I graduated in 2024 with a Bachelor of Arts in Anthropology and spent the following months seeking work. I landed a job working for a subsidary of eBay, Goldin Auctions, where I am a Historical Cataloger and Researcher. I find this work fulfilling, though it can prove to be extremely hectic. My work has provided me with the opportunity to combine a number of my passions while also being able to travel to new places.

My free time mostly consists of exploring South Philly, where I have now lived for just under a year, trying new foods and beverages at every available opportunity. I enjoy hiking up in the White Mountains of New Hampshire with the hope of completing the 48 4,000 footers one day. I often bounce around the Tri-State area visiting family and friends as well. I have a deep love and passion for sports, I follow all Philly teams aside from the Flyers as I am a Hershey Bears season ticket hold and by externsion, a Washington Capitals fan for life.
