Associations, Organizations and Other Links

Currently, I am a member of the ALA (American Library Association) and hope to continue my involvement with the organization. I hope in the future to become a member of the AAA (American Anthropological Association).

Additionally, I am a member of a charity organization created by a few friends of mine from Temple Universty, the Culinary Arts Clubt of Temple or CART for short. Our mission is to provide meals to less fortunate and senior members of the Philadelphia community by organizing giveaways in neighborhoods such as Kensington and coordinating with churches and senior communities to make lunches for those who may not be able to cook for themselves.
