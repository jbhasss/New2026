Welcome to the world of Pokemon TCG!

The Pokémon Trading Card Game has connected players and collectors for nearly three decades. What began as a simple card game based on a popular video game franchise has grown into one of the largest trading card hobbies in the world. Today, millions of people participate in local tournaments, attend trade shows, build collections, and connect with others who share a passion for Pokémon. This website serves as a community resource for both experienced enthusiasts and newcomers. Whether you are interested in learning how to play, improving your deck-building skills, finding information about older card sets, or exploring unique collections from around the world, this site is designed to provide useful information and connect members of the community.

What is Pokémon TCG?

The Pokémon TCG appeals to a wide variety of people because it offers multiple ways to participate. Some players enjoy the strategic side of competitive tournaments, while others focus on collecting artwork, completing sets, or preserving rare cards. Many community members enjoy both aspects of the hobby.

Why Should I Be Involved?

Pokemon popularity is quickly reaching an all time high in both competitive play and collecting communities. Cards are going for thousands upon thousands of dollars while records continue to be broken in the community. As of February of 2026, a Pokemon card officially set the record for the most expensive card ever sold at auction for a whopping 16 million dollars! If you want to get in on some of the monetary action, the time to do so is now. On the other hand, with so many new games having been released in the last decade, the competitive field evolves as well. New decks released with each generation continue to offer players new and unique ways to build decks and battle.
