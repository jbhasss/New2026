Collecting and Grading Information

Collecting Pokémon cards is a popular aspect of the hobby, with many enthusiasts seeking rare and valuable cards to add to their collections. Grading is an important part of card collecting, as it helps determine the condition and value of a card. Professional grading companies such as PSA and BGS evaluate cards based on factors such as centering, corners, edges, and surface quality.

Professional grading services evaluate cards and assign a numerical grade based on condition. Graded cards are then sealed in protective holders that help preserve authenticity and condition. Many collectors choose to grade cards because it provides an independent assessment and can increase buyer confidence when selling or trading.

Small imperfections can significantly affect a card's value, particularly when dealing with rare or highly sought-after cards. If you happen to have a store of 'raw' cards, you can help to prevent blemishes by placing them in sleeves or better yet, plastic mag holders

Understanding Card Conditions

Collectors typically evaluate cards based on:

-Surface quality: Blemishes on foil, missing colors in art cards and similar aspects

-Edge wear: Worn edges where the cardboard has peeled or faded.

-Corner condition: Damage to the corners of the card, wearing or other tears that can impact a grade.

-Centering: How well the image is centered on the card, a common issue among newer cards.

Some companies grade much more strictly than others, so when choosing to grade your cards do your due dilligence. Keep in mind also that although everyone has their own aesthetic when it comes to slabs, certain companies are held in higher regard. Most people tend to go strictly PSA, although BGS is not too far behind.

Grading can be a costly process, especially for high-value cards. It's important to weigh the potential increase in value against the cost of grading before submitting cards for evaluation. Additionally, the grading process can take several weeks or even months, so patience is key when waiting for results. PSA recently announced they would be shutting down their Value Bulk intake as the wait time has grown from 2 to 6 months in the last year alone!
