Trade Shows & Expos

Trade shows are one of the most exciting parts of the Pokémon collecting community. Unlike online marketplaces, trade shows allow collectors to see cards in person, evaluate condition firsthand, and negotiate directly with other collectors and vendors. These events can range from small local gatherings to large regional or national conventions. They often feature a wide variety of cards, including rare and vintage items that may not be available online.

Large events often attract attendees from across the country and feature hundreds of tables filled with cards, sealed products, memorabilia, and collectibles. Many events also host guest appearances, giveaways, and educational sessions focused on collecting and card preservation.

Benefits of Attending Trade Shows

Trade shows provide opportunities to:

-Buy cards directly from vendors

-Trade with fellow collectors

-Learn about market trends

-Discover rare and unusual items

-Meet people who share similar collecting interests

Preparing for an Event

Collectors should research event schedules ahead of time, establish a budget, and create a list of cards they hope to find. Bringing cards organized in binders or protective cases can also make trading easier and more efficient. Budgeting for these events can often prove to be difficult, and it is a fairly standard practice at shows for cards not to sell for full price, but much closer to 80% of the total value. Even then, most values are determined by the most recent sale of the card, which can depend on the grade if the card is slabbed.
